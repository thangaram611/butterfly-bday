Use: the background of the wide preview picture shown when the birthday website's link is shared in WhatsApp. Image 1 is the style reference sheet: match its paper, cut edges, shadows and palette exactly. Image 2 is the approved dawn garden: draw the same garden, with the same sky, hills, wisteria, meadow, daisies and blossoms, re-composed for a wide frame.

Format: wide landscape 3:2.

Scene, from back to front:
1. Sky: a soft paper gradient from off-white at the top to pale lilac near the horizon, with the pale gold paper sun low on the left, just above the hills.
2. Far hills: overlapping rolling hills in pale lavender and amethyst right across the frame.
3. Wisteria: drooping clusters of lilac and amethyst wisteria hanging from indigo vines in the top-right corner, and a small one in the top-left corner.
4. Meadow: a rolling violet meadow across the lower part, dotted with small off-white and lilac paper flowers with gold centres and a few tall violet grass blades.
5. Foreground blossoms: large indigo and violet paper flowers and leaves very close to the viewer in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners, softly out of focus.

Keep the middle third of the frame calm and simple, only sky, hills and meadow: a girl will be placed standing there later. Keep the upper-left sky open and pale: a headline will sit there. The top and bottom tenth of the frame will be trimmed off, so keep everything that matters inside the middle band. No butterflies, no people, no animals.
