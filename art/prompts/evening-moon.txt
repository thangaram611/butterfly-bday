Use: the evening backdrop behind the last two screens of a children's birthday invitation website; white text sits over its middle, so nothing bright may be there. Image 1 is the current evening garden. Image 2 is the style reference sheet.

Format: the same tall portrait 2:3 frame as image 1, fully opaque.

Task: recreate image 1 exactly, with the same composition, wisteria, lanterns, string lights, stars, hills, meadow and foreground blossoms in the same places, sizes and colours. Change only the moon: make it about two thirds of its current size and set it lower, so that only its top third peeks above the far hills, and keep its glow faint. The sky where the moon was becomes the same calm dark dusk as the rest of the sky.
